## Gioi Thieu Mon Hoc

**Xu Ly Tin Hieu So (Digital Signal Processing - DSP)** la linh vuc nghien cuu cac phuong phap xu ly tin hieu thong qua he thong so hoa. Phan lon tin hieu trong thuc te ton tai o dang tuong tu (analog) - lien tuc theo thoi gian va bien do - va can duoc chuyen doi sang dang so thong qua qua trinh lay mau va luong tu hoa de may tinh co the xu ly duoc.

DSP ket hop kien thuc tu nhieu linh vuc: toan hoc (giai tich, dai so tuyen tinh), ky thuat dien tu so, ly thuyet xac suat va thong tin, xu ly tin hieu tuong tu. Su phat trien cua phan cung so hien dai (vi xu ly DSP, FPGA, vi dieu khien) da bien DSP tro thanh nen tang cua hang loat cong nghe: vien thong, y sinh, xu ly am thanh va hinh anh, radar, dinh vi ve tinh, va nhieu he thong ky thuat khac.

Sau khi hoan thanh mon hoc, sinh vien co kha nang:

- Giai thich khai niem tin hieu va he thong, hieu nguyen ly hoat dong cua he thong xu ly tin hieu so.
- Mo ta khai niem, tinh chat va ung dung cua bien doi Z trong phan tich he thong so.
- Phan tich tin hieu va he thong trong mien tan so su dung bien doi Fourier.

## Noi Dung Mon Hoc

### Chuong 1 - Introduction to Digital Signal Processing

Tong quan ve DSP: ban chat tin hieu tuong tu va tin hieu so, quy trinh chuyen doi Analog-to-Digital (ADC) va Digital-to-Analog (DAC). Phan tich uu nhuoc diem cua xu ly tin hieu so so voi xu ly tuong tu. Gioi thieu cac ung dung DSP trong thuc te: vien thong, y te, quan su, cong nghiep, o to, khong gian.

### Chuong 2 - Signal and System in Time Domain

Tin hieu va he thong trong mien thoi gian: cac tin hieu roi rac co ban (xung don vi, buoc don vi, tin hieu doc, tin hieu mui), tinh chat he thong tuyen tinh bat bien theo thoi gian (LTI), tich chap (convolution), phan tich he thong thong qua dap ung xung.

### Chuong 3 - Z-Transform

Bien doi Z va bien doi Z nguoc: dinh nghia, vung hoi tu (ROC), tinh chat, phan tich he thong LTI bang bien doi Z, phuong trinh sai phan (difference equation), thiet ke bo loc so co ban.

### Chuong 4 - Signal and System in Frequency Domain

Bien doi Fourier thoi gian roi rac (DTFT): dinh nghia, tinh chat, dap ung tan so cua he thong LTI, loc tuyen tinh trong mien tan so, tieu chuan Nyquist va hien tuong aliasing.

### Chuong 5 - The Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT)

Bien doi Fourier roi rac (DFT) va bien doi nhanh Fourier (FFT): dinh nghia, tinh chat, moi quan he voi DTFT, ung dung DFT trong phan tich pho tin hieu, loc trong mien tan so.
